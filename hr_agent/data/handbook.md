# Acme Employee Handbook (sample)

## PTO accrual
Full-time employees accrue 1.67 PTO days per month (20 days per year). Part-time employees
accrue PTO pro rata to scheduled hours. Accrual starts on the first day of employment.

## PTO carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days carry over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on
March 31 of the new year. Unused days above the cap are forfeited on December 31.

## PTO requests and approval
Submit PTO requests at least 2 weeks ahead for absences of 3 or more consecutive days.
Requests are approved by the employee's manager. Approval is not automatic; a request stays
pending until the manager acts.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 40 hours of sick leave per calendar year, available on day one. Sick leave
does not carry over. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days.

## Parental leave
Birth and adoptive parents receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave, taken within 12 months
of the child's arrival. Leave may be taken in one block or split into two blocks.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager agreement. Working from
another country for more than 14 days per year requires HR approval for tax and legal reasons.

## Expense reimbursement
Business expenses under 75 USD need a receipt photo; larger expenses need an itemized
receipt and manager approval. Claims are due within 30 days and are paid with the next
payroll run.

## Payroll schedule
Employees are paid monthly on the last business day of the month. Payroll corrections
reported after the run closes are paid in the following month's run.
